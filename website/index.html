An operating system that can explain its own failures.

Psydian is a small kernel written from scratch in Rust. When something breaks, it records what happened, and you can ask an AI why, in plain English. The AI can suggest commands, but it can never run them.

psydian ai

About

A real kernel, with an assistant that stays outside it.

Psydian boots on bare x86-64 hardware (or QEMU) and gives you a simple shell. It was built as a college project to explore one question: how can AI help with low-level failures without being trusted with the system?

01

Written from scratch

Descriptor tables, interrupts, paging, a kernel heap, keyboard and serial input, and a shell. No borrowed operating system underneath.

02

Failures become records

Faults are caught and turned into structured diagnostics, with a code, a subsystem and context, instead of a frozen screen.

03

Ask in plain English

Type ai why did the heap fail? and get a short explanation with next steps, usually in about two seconds.

04

You stay in charge

Suggested commands carry a risk level. Nothing runs until you type run, and destructive commands are blocked outright.

How it works

The kernel never touches the network.

A separate program on your computer, the AI Bridge, sits between the kernel and the AI service. It is the only part that knows the API key.

AI service

Free Gemini by default. OmniRoute, OpenRouter or a local Ollama model also work.

Diagnostic data is wrapped and labelled so that instructions hidden inside it are ignored. Answers are cut to fixed sizes and plain ASCII before they reach the kernel.

Try it

Install

On Linux or macOS, in a terminal. On Windows, run wsl --install first and use the Ubuntu terminal.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Itx-Psycho0/PsyDian_OS/main/scripts/install.sh | bash

Choose an AI service

Pick Gemini and paste a free key from Google AI Studio. It is saved only on your computer.

psydian ai-setup

Boot and ask

Break something on purpose, then ask why.

psydian ai
psydian> fault-test heap
psydian> ai why did the heap fail?
psydian> run 1

No key? psydian demo boots with canned AI answers. Other commands: psydian check, psydian test, psydian help.
